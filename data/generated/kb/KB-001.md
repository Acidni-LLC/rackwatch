# Port-channel flapping on Cisco 9200L access switches

Symptom: %ETHCHNL-5-PORT_STATUS flaps and hosts behind the switch drop.
Check the SFP optics first; a bad optic mimics a config fault.
Do NOT factory reset the switch: it wipes the access VLANs and is never approved.
